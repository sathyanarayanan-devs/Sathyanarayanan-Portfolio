My Top 3 moview of all Time

Baasha

Baasha is a classic Tamil movie that has stood the test of time. Rajinikanth's performance in this film is legendary, and the story is engaging from start to finish.

Vikram

Vikram is a movie that has a great storyline and amazing action sequences. Kamal Haasan's performance is outstanding in this film.

Master

Master is a movie that showcases the talent of Vijay and Vijay Sethupathi. The film has a gripping plot and excellent music. It is a must-watch for action movie lovers.
